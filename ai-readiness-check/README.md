# AI Readiness Check

*Diagnóstico de preparación para IA*

A 3-minute check of whether your small business is ready for AI. It has 10 questions across 5 pillars: strategy, data, talent, infrastructure and governance.

You get a score out of 30, a tier (Laggard, Follower, Chaser or Pacesetter), a bar for each pillar, and three concrete first steps for your weakest pillar.

Everything runs in your browser. There's no sign-up and no backend.

**Live:** https://rantunano.github.io/smb-ai-toolkit/ai-readiness-check/

The benchmarks come from the Cisco AI Readiness Index, Mexico results (220 organizations), as reported by DPL News in October 2026: 4% Pacesetters, 39% Chasers, 42% Followers and 15% Laggards. Your score is a self-assessment and does not reproduce Cisco's methodology.
