# AI Depth Ladder

*Escalera de profundidad de IA*

Is AI in your team's browser tabs, or in your business processes?

1. **List your AI uses**: what it's used for, how often and by how many people, and whether it's documented.
2. **See your rung** for each use: Experimenting, Some tasks, Regular & shared, or Built in. You also get the next step to climb.
3. **Turn your best candidate into a one-page AI SOP** (approved prompt, inputs, reviewer, data never to paste, and a 30-day metric), then print it or copy it.

Runs 100% in your browser. No sign-up, no backend, no tracking. Your draft is saved only in your own browser (localStorage).

**Live:** https://YOUR-USER.github.io/smb-ai-toolkit/ai-depth-ladder/

## Why
In Gusto's *2026 State of Small Business* survey (1,731 U.S. small businesses with fewer than 25 employees, Aug–Sep 2026), 67% use AI tools. But only 15% use them regularly across several functions, and only 5% say AI is built into every part of the business. Source: [Gusto via PR Newswire, Nov 12 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/small-business-owners-are-more-confident-in-their-own-companies-than-in-the-economy-and-two-thirds-use-ai-gusto-survey-finds-302936587.html).

## How rungs are assigned
| Rung | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1. Experimenting | Tried once or twice |
| 2. Some tasks | Monthly, or weekly/daily by one person |
| 3. Regular & shared | Weekly/daily by 2+ people |
| 4. Built in | Rung 3, plus a written prompt, defined inputs, a named reviewer, and it's the default way |

The **candidate** is the rung-3 use with the most usage (frequency × people). If there's none, it's the busiest rung-2 use.
